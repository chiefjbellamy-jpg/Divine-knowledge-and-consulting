Two kinds of work, one standard

One-on-one spiritual care from an ordained minister and chaplain who works across traditions. Whatever your path — any faith, many paths, or none — you are met where you are, without judgment and without steering. Support, reflection, and guidance — in your tradition, at your pace.

People of every tradition — and no tradition — are equally welcome here. Your beliefs lead; mine never steer.

  • Spiritual clarity sessions — explore purpose, values, questions of belief and meaning, and personal direction
  • Life-transition guidance — support and reflection through grief, relationship changes, career shifts, and other major decisions
  • Spiritual-practice planning — build a personal routine around practices you choose, such as prayer, meditation, or journaling
  • Beliefs and values consultation — align your goals and decisions with your own beliefs and values
  • Support for first responders, veterans, and caregivers

You choose whether to include spiritual practices — nothing is ever imposed. Spiritual consultation is companionship and care. It complements — and never replaces — licensed counseling, medical, or mental-health treatment, and anyone who needs diagnosis or treatment is referred to a licensed mental-health professional.

How we work — HEART

H — Hear and understand. Listening first, with whole attention.
E — Expect the best. Of people, and of every outcome — even the lesson in a failure.
A — Act with integrity. The right thing, even when no one is looking.
R — Respect diversity. Every tradition honored; respect does not require agreement.
T — Transcend yourself. See the situation from more than one side of the wall.

Boundaries — stated clearly

Our words are support, reflection, and guidance. Before we work together, these boundaries are stated plainly:

  • We don't diagnose or treat mental-health conditions — that is licensed work, and we refer people who need it to licensed professionals.
  • We don't claim to represent every person or community associated with a belief.
  • We don't pressure clients to adopt, reject, or disclose a belief or practice.
  • We ask permission before discussing spiritual or cultural topics, and we respect a client's right to disagree or opt out.
  • We explain confidentiality, fees, credentials, and referral practices before working together.

You choose whether spiritual practices are part of your sessions; our beliefs are never imposed on you, and we offer only the services we are qualified to provide.

About Jonathan Bellamy

Jonathan Bellamy began in the fire service at 16 and rose to Fire Chief of the Southwest Volunteer Fire Department (2008–2022), where he built a dual-response paid-and-volunteer model and coordinated air and ground medical transport at a major train derailment. He is an EMT, a crisis mediator who has served with Florida Emergency Management and FEMA disaster assistance teams, an ordained minister whose chaplaincy and spiritual care have served people of many traditions — and none. He is also a French-trained chef.

After losing his parents and brother, he rebuilt his life around a single conviction: tell the truth, and hand people back their own decisions. That conviction became Fresh Jon Innovations — The Divine Mirror, The Divine Recipe, and Divine Knowledge Counseling and Consulting.

He also speaks to organizations and groups on trust, resilience, and truth.

"Never stand still: learn daily and lead boldly."
