About Jonathan Bellamy

Jonathan Bellamy began in the fire service at 16 and rose to Fire Chief of the Southwest Volunteer Fire Department (2008–2022), where he built a dual-response paid-and-volunteer model and coordinated air and ground medical transport at a major train derailment. He is an EMT, a crisis mediator who has served with Florida Emergency Management and FEMA disaster assistance teams, an ordained non-denominational minister focused on chaplaincy and spiritual care — and a French-trained chef.

After losing his parents and brother, he rebuilt his life around a single conviction: tell the truth, and hand people back their own decisions. That conviction became Fresh Jon Innovations — The Divine Mirror, The Divine Recipe, and Divine Knowledge & Consulting.

He also speaks to organizations and groups on trust, resilience, and truth.

"Never stand still: learn daily and lead boldly."
